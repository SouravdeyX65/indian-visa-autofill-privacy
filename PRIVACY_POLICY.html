Privacy Policy - Indian Visa Autofill (UNOFFICIAL)

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Indian Visa Autofill (UNOFFICIAL) is an independent browser extension. It is not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by the Government of India or any visa authority.

Information the extension handles

The extension handles information that users choose to enter or import, which may include applicant and passport details, profile information, preferences, document files, PDF content, and screenshots. This information can be sensitive personal information.

Local storage and processing

Profiles and settings are stored in Chrome extension storage on the user's device. When enabled by the user, saved PDFs and screenshots are stored in the extension's local database (IndexedDB). PDF text extraction, document/photo processing, and the bundled OCR processing are performed in the browser on the user's device. The extension does not operate its own account system or profile-data server.

CAPTCHA recognition and third-party service

The optional CAPTCHA helper first attempts recognition on the user's device using OCR components bundled with the extension. If its local result is not sufficiently reliable, the CAPTCHA image may be sent over HTTPS to OCR.space (api.ocr.space) for text recognition. Only the CAPTCHA image is sent for this fallback; the extension's profile, passport details, and other form fields are not intentionally included in that request. OCR.space is an independent third party and its handling of submitted images is governed by its own policies. Users who do not use the CAPTCHA helper do not trigger this transfer.

Sharing and sale

The extension developer does not receive, sell, or share locally stored profile information. The only described third-party transfer is the optional CAPTCHA-image fallback to OCR.space. The extension does not include advertising or analytics trackers.

Backups, PDFs and screenshots

Users may export a JSON backup. Exported backups are not encrypted by the extension and may contain sensitive information; keep them private. If the user enables the option to keep PDFs and screenshots, those files remain in local extension storage until deleted. Screenshots are captured only after the user invokes the screenshot feature. Users can open, download, or delete saved files from the extension's Files area.

User controls and deletion

Users can edit or delete profiles and saved files in the extension. The extension's delete-all control removes locally stored extension data. Uninstalling the extension or clearing Chrome data may also remove locally stored information. Exported backup files must be deleted separately by the user.

Accuracy and application submission

The extension is an assistance tool. It does not submit visa applications, make payments, guarantee eligibility, or guarantee that extracted or populated information is accurate. Users must independently verify all information and submit applications themselves.
